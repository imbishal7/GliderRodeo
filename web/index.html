Glider Rodeo 2026 · Kaena Ridge, Oahu

Noise Nowcaster

Predicts how loud the ocean is from the weather and sea state, and what that does to a glider's listening range.

Conditions

The model never saw this glider during training: each glider is predicted by a model fitted on the other five. Values are decibels relative to that glider's own median.

Permutation importance measures how much accuracy is lost when one input is shuffled. depth_rate_ms is the glider's own dive activity, so importance there is self-noise rather than weather.
